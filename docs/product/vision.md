# Trovato AI - Product Vision

## Mission

Help travelers effortlessly discover and secure tickets for museums, attractions, exhibitions, festivals, and experiences before they sell out.

---

## Vision

Searching for attraction tickets across multiple websites is fragmented, time-consuming, and often results in discovering that tickets are already sold out.

Trovato AI simplifies this process by providing an AI-powered assistant that understands natural language, searches available providers, recommends alternatives, and proactively alerts users when tickets become available.

---

## Target Users

- Tourists
- Families
- Solo travellers
- Digital nomads
- Frequent city explorers

---

## MVP Goals

The first version of Trovato AI focuses on validating the core value proposition:

- AI-powered conversational search
- Attraction discovery
- Ticket availability
- Alternative recommendations
- Availability alerts
- User authentication
- Push notifications

---

## Long-Term Vision

Become the AI travel assistant that helps users discover, plan, and book memorable experiences with confidence.
